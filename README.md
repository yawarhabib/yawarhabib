## Bio

Plant geneticist from India, based in Moscow. I am a Research Scientist at the Center for Bio and Medical Technologies, Skolkovo Institute of Science and Technology (Skoltech), where I also did my PhD (2021–2025) on the quantitative and molecular genetics of wheat and soybean. Before that I studied botany at Aligarh Muslim University (BSc, MSc).

My work sits between the field and the keyboard: multi-environment breeding trials, linear mixed models, genome-wide association studies (GWAS), population genetics, genomic prediction and marker-assisted selection, with R as the main tool. I care about analyses that someone else can run from start to finish, so my papers ship with their code and data.

Outside research I make short science videos about genetics, and I am building *From Seed to Code*, a bilingual project on plant science and programming for biologists in Russia and India.

## Research

- Statistical analysis of plant breeding trials: linear mixed models, unbalanced and multi-year designs, heritability, genotypic values
- Two-stage GWAS and the weighting of adjusted means from unbalanced field trials
- Population genetics of crop germplasm collections: diversity, structure and subpopulations
- Genomic prediction and marker-assisted selection for grain quality and freezing tolerance in durum wheat
- Multi-trait selection indices and ideotype design for soybean (MGIDI, MTSI); rapid generation advancement
- Crops: bread wheat, durum wheat and soybean

## Software

- [**rotationGWAS**](https://github.com/yawarhabib/rotationGWAS) — an R package for the second stage of a two-stage GWAS. It carries the full variance–covariance matrix of the adjusted means into the marker model by the rotation of Piepho et al. (2012), so that an unbalanced trial is analysed with the right weights. With Hans-Peter Piepho. [Documentation](https://yawarhabib.github.io/rotationGWAS/)
